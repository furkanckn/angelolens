What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. This policy explains which cookies angelolens.com uses.

Cookies we use

Strictly necessary / preference: NEXT_LOCALE stores your language choice for up to one year so the site opens in your preferred language. Essential technical cookies may be used for security and basic operation.

Analytics and marketing

We do not currently use third-party advertising or analytics cookies. If this changes, we will update this policy and obtain consent where required.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the language cookie may mean your locale preference is not remembered.
